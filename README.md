# LED-Grid
A simple battery-powered LED grid a friend asked me to design for them, so they could put a vinyl decoration on top for conventions and stuff. The BOM included is for JLC, since it's what this prototype is designed for. Just plug a battery in and on your merry way :D

It's meant to be housed on a (yet to design) 3D printed case that will have a slot on the front for interchangeable vinyls to display as a con badge. It's powerful enough to blind you at short distance, and should be pretty good at dissipating heat. just make sure your battery is above 2500mAh and you'll be good for around 8 hours or so! :D
